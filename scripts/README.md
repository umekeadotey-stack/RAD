## 📖 WRITING TIPS FOR SCRIPTS

1. **Use conversational language** - Write like you speak
2. **Keep sentences short** - Easy to read while filming
3. **Use numbers & lists** - Makes content scannable
4. **Add emotional hooks** - Tell stories, not facts
5. **Include pauses** - [PAUSE] for emphasis
6. **Mark emphasis** - *highlight important points*
7. **Natural transitions** - "So here's the thing..." instead of "Next point"

---

## 🎤 RECORDING NOTES

When recording from your script:
- Don't read word-for-word - use it as a guide
- Look at the camera (pretend you're talking to a friend)
- Speak clearly and slightly slower than normal
- Take breaks between sections
- Do multiple takes if needed
- Record in a quiet space
- Good lighting is crucial
