## 💼 Income Streams Breakdown

### Passive Income Streams
1. **Digital Products** (Ebooks, templates, courses)
   - Effort: High upfront, Low ongoing
   - Income: Recurring
   - Tools: Gumroad, Teachable, Kajabi

2. **Affiliate Marketing**
   - Effort: Medium
   - Income: Per sale commission
   - Tools: Amazon Associates, ShareASale

3. **Advertising Revenue**
   - Effort: Low (after initial content)
   - Income: CPM-based
   - Tools: YouTube AdSense, Mediavine

4. **Membership Communities**
   - Effort: Medium ongoing
   - Income: Recurring subscriptions
   - Tools: Circle, Mighty Networks, Patreon

5. **Rental Income & Assets**
   - Effort: Medium setup, Low ongoing
   - Income: Recurring
   - Tools: Airbnb, Turo, etc.

### Active Income Streams
1. **Freelancing** (Fiverr, Upwork)
2. **Consulting/Coaching** (1-on-1 services)
3. **Content Creation** (YouTube, sponsorships)
4. **Blogging** (Affiliate + AdSense)
5. **Courses/Workshops** (Teaching)
